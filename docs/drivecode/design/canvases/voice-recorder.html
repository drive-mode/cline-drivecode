Voice recorder · Drive demo "You" lines

Record each line, play it back, re-record until happy, then Download. Say it naturally — the demo's on-screen text will be synced to whatever you actually say.

Tips: quiet room, ~15cm from the mic, start speaking half a second after pressing Record. Chrome/Edge required (uses MediaRecorder). Files download as .webm; they'll be transcoded for the demo.

When done: the files land in your Downloads folder as you-01…you-05.webm. Drop them into docs/drivecode/assets/demos/voice/raw/ (or just tell Claude where they are) and they'll be levelled, transcoded, and wired into the demo as your voice.
