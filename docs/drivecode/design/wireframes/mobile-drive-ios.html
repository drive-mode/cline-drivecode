Modern light rules

  • Page #F8FAFB, cards #FFFFFF, ink #151516 @ 78% for body — brand light tokens, not gray-on-gray.
  • Violet fills CTAs and live hero; washes for icons/selection — never purple-washed page chrome.
  • Depth from soft shadows + 8% hairlines; glass only on floating tab bar.
  • Typography: Schibsted Grotesk, heavy display (−0.045em), 15–16px interactive, IBM Plex Mono for diffs.
  • Theme toggle flips the same five surfaces — light is default, dark is peer.
